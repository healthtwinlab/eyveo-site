Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27.11.2026 · Version 0.1 (test phase). In case of doubt, the German version prevails.

This privacy policy applies to the EYVEO app (Android, iOS) and the website eyveo.app. It explains which personal data we process, why, on what legal basis, who receives it and what rights you have.

1. Controller

Ivan Sudak, sole proprietor (Einzelunternehmer) · Schillerstraße 22, 06712 Zeitz, Germany · Email: datenschutz@eyveo.app · Phone: +49 1521 1096249

We have not appointed a data protection officer because, in our assessment, the legal requirements (Art. 37 GDPR, Section 38 BDSG) are not met. Please send privacy requests to the email address above.

2. Summary

  • Your data is stored in the EU (data centre in Frankfurt am Main, Germany).
  • The camera image used by EVision+ is analysed only on your device and is never uploaded.
  • We process health-related information only with your explicit consent. Each consent is separate, voluntary and can be withdrawn in the app at any time.
  • AI features are provided by Google. We do not store photos sent for AI analysis.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or build advertising profiles.
  • The app is intended for people aged 16 and over.

3. User account

Data: email address, password (stored only as a hash), optionally your name, time of registration and sign-in. Purpose: creating and managing your account, sign-in, password reset, syncing your data across devices. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract for the use of the app). Account and password emails are sent via our hosting provider (section 13).

4. Profile, training and nutrition

Data: sex, age, height, current and target weight, activity level, training goals (including in your own words), training location, available equipment (“My Gym”), training plans, completed sets, reps and weights, detected technique errors (as text and percentage), food diary, water intake, diet type, pantry items for EYVEO Chef, supplements you entered and their intake. Purpose: creating and adapting your training and nutrition recommendations, progress display, reminders. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Which of these items are sent to the AI is described in section 6.

5. Health-related data (only with consent)

Some information may reveal details about your health and is specially protected (Art. 9 GDPR). We process it only if you have explicitly consented in the app (Art. 9(2)(a) in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

The app keeps working without these consents; only the related feature is unavailable. You can withdraw any consent under Profile → Data and account → Consents. After withdrawal we delete the affected data; processing before the withdrawal remains lawful (Art. 7(3) GDPR). We record when you gave or withdrew consent so that we can demonstrate it (Art. 7(1) GDPR).

6. AI features

EYVEO uses artificial intelligence. AI content is labelled as EyveoCoach or “AI analysis”. EyveoCoach is an AI system, not a human.

Feature What is sent to the AI What we store
EyveoCoach (chat) your question, optionally a photo; profile, goals, recent workouts, detected technique errors, today’s nutrition; allergies and well-being/supplements only with consent (section 5) chat history in your account until deleted; photo not stored

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for health-related information, your consent under section 5. Provider: Google (section 13). Currently: Google Gemini API; processing may take place outside the EU (section 14). Under the terms of the paid service, Google does not use your inputs to train its models; Google may log inputs for a limited period to detect abuse.

Please note: AI answers may be wrong. They are general fitness and nutrition information and do not replace advice from a doctor, therapist or dietitian. No automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

Photos and videos of other people: please do not record other people, especially in a gym, and follow your gym’s house rules.

7. Camera: EVision+ (technique detection)

EVision+ analyses the camera image only on your device (Google ML Kit, running locally). The video is not stored and not transmitted to us or third parties. Only the results are saved to your account: number of reps, detected technique errors and percentages. Still images are stored only with your consent (section 5). The camera is used only after you grant the system permission, which you can revoke in your device settings at any time.

8. Voice input

Voice input uses your operating system’s speech recognition (Android: Google, iOS: Apple). Depending on your device settings, the audio may be processed in the cloud by the operating system provider under its own privacy terms. EYVEO receives only the recognised text and does not store audio. Microphone permission is optional; you can type instead.

9. Health Connect and Apple Health

If you enable the connection, EYVEO reads: steps, active calories, heart rate, sleep and weight. EYVEO does not write data to Health Connect or Apple Health. The data is displayed and evaluated only on your device; it is not sent to our servers or to the AI, not used for advertising and not sold to third parties. Use of data from Health Connect complies with the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including its limited use requirements. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time via the system permissions.

10. Notifications, sharing and exercise videos

  • Reminders (e.g. supplement intake) are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Sharing: you share achievements yourself through your device’s share menu. The card contains no health data (no body weight, no limitations).
  • Exercise videos are downloaded from our storage in Frankfurt the first time you play them; this technically involves your IP address (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in delivering the content).

11. Test phase

During the test phase you can send us feedback by email or messenger. We use it only to improve the app and delete it after evaluation, at the latest 6 months after the end of the test phase. See the separate tester information.

12. Website

When you visit eyveo.app, our hosting provider processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system, in order to deliver the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising services. Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA; EU-US Data Privacy Framework / Standard Contractual Clauses). The domain is managed by Hostinger International Ltd. (Cyprus, EU).

Fonts, images and videos on this website are hosted on our own web space and are not loaded from third parties. The home page uses its own script solely to animate the logo; it does not store anything on your device, does not read any data and does not load any content. The website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or tracking services.

14. Transfers to third countries

Where data reaches countries outside the EU/EEA (USA, Singapore), we rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where the recipient is certified) or on EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). You can request a copy of these safeguards.

16. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). An informal email to datenschutz@eyveo.app is enough.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). Our competent authority is: Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Sachsen-Anhalt, Otto-von-Guericke-Straße 34a, 39104 Magdeburg, Germany, email: poststelle@lfd.sachsen-anhalt.de, datenschutz.sachsen-anhalt.de.

17. Minimum age

EYVEO is intended for people aged 16 and over. When registering, you confirm that you are at least 16. If we learn that an account was created by a younger person, we delete it.

18. Required data

An account requires an email address and a password. All other information is voluntary; without it, recommendations are less accurate.

19. Security

Data is transmitted encrypted (TLS). Each user can technically access only their own data (database-level access rules). Files such as technique error images are kept in private storage.

20. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or the law changes. We inform you in the app about material changes and ask separately for any new consent.
