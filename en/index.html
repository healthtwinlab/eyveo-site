Features

EYVEO – your trainer and nutrition coach.

Helps you with training, form and choosing your nutrition. An app that knows you and learns with you every day.

NEW

Vision+

Your phone becomes your coach. Set it down, train – Vision+ counts along and corrects you in real time.

  • Counts reps automatically
  • Spots typical mistakes – like knees caving in or a rounded back
  • Voice cues, no need to look at the screen
  • Image analysis runs entirely on your phone

3D form for every exercise

Every exercise as a 3D video – done right and with typical mistakes. The working muscles light up.

EyveoCoach

Your AI coach knows your training, nutrition and goals – and, if you allow it, your daily readiness – and answers in plain words.

Nutrition from a photo

Snap your plate: estimated calories, protein, fat, carbs and fibre – always editable.

EYVEO Chef

Scan your groceries and get menus and recipes made only from what you have at home – matched to your training and your goal.

My Gym

Film your gym for a few seconds – the AI recognises the equipment. Plans use only what is really there.

Supplements on track

Plan and tick off your intake, with information from open, recognised sources. No dosage advice.

Privacy

Your data stays yours.

We build EYVEO in Germany and take data protection seriously.

Live camera image stays on your phoneVision+ analyses your movement directly on the device; the video is not uploaded. We only save single frames of form mistakes if you allow it. Photos and short videos you take for the AI (meal, fridge, gym) are sent for analysis and not stored permanently.
Database in FrankfurtYour account and training data are stored with our provider Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany. For AI features we use Google Gemini; processing may currently also take place outside the EU.
Delete your account anytimeIn the app or by email – with all related data.
This website uses no cookiesNo trackers, no ads, no external content.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When will EYVEO be available?

A small group is testing the app right now. Android comes first, iOS follows. Write to us if you want to join.

How much does EYVEO cost?

We will announce pricing before launch. During the beta, the app is free for testers.

Does EYVEO replace a doctor or physiotherapist?

No. EYVEO is a fitness and wellness coach and does not give medical advice. If you have complaints or health conditions, please talk to your doctor first.

Do I need a gym?

Not necessarily – we suggest exercises for home, without equipment or with whatever you have at home. The “My Gym” feature adapts your training plan to what you have available.

Which languages does EYVEO support?

German and English at launch, more languages will follow.

How do I become a beta tester?

Send a short email to hello@eyveo.app with your phone model. We will get back to you.
