Delete your EYVEO account

To delete your EYVEO account in the app: Profile → Data and account → Delete account → confirm. This deletes your account and all related data: profile, workouts, food diary, check-ins, supplements, consents and saved images.

Without the app: email datenschutz@eyveo.app from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete account". We delete your account within 30 days and confirm the deletion by email.

Backups are overwritten after 30 days at the latest. We keep only data we are legally required to keep (for example records of consent), and only as long as necessary.
